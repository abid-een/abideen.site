## Risk Mitigation

### High-Risk Areas
1. **User Adoption**: Complex workflows may affect usability
2. **Training Effectiveness**: Materials may not meet user needs
3. **Support Capacity**: Support team may be overwhelmed
4. **Documentation Quality**: Documentation may be incomplete or unclear

### Mitigation Strategies
1. **User Adoption**: Excellent training, responsive support, iterative improvements
2. **Training Effectiveness**: User feedback, iterative improvement, multiple formats
3. **Support Capacity**: Proper staffing, automation, escalation procedures
4. **Documentation Quality**: Peer review, user testing, regular updates
